I have spent 15 years building web and mobile products, most of it inside international startups and scale-ups in Berlin and Copenhagen. Since 2023 I have built and launched four AI products of my own.

## About me {#about}

I plan, architect and build digital products end to end. Most of my career has gone into international startups and scale-ups, leading teams, owning the architecture decisions and helping early-stage companies grow into working businesses.

I have worked with AI since OpenAI released GPT-3, and I have built and launched four AI products of my own — including [SQLAI.ai](https://www.sqlai.ai), which is profitable and has over 150,000 users. That work covers the practical side of applied AI: RAG, embeddings and vector search, prompt design and AI-assisted development. The aim each time is to solve a real business problem, not to produce another demo.

Before that came a decade of client work: automotive marketplaces, crypto banking, e-learning, delivery apps and Danish public-sector platforms. From front-end architecture and design systems to APIs, search and geospatial data.

## How I work {#approach}

- **Business first.** The startup years taught me to ask what a feature is meant to achieve before asking how to build it.
- **Simple, testable code.** Code is read far more often than it is written. I pick the solution the next developer can follow.
- **Ownership over process.** I lead for results and personal responsibility rather than micro-management and ceremony.
- **An eye for UX.** I have designed my own products from first sketch to paying customers, and I take the user experience as seriously as the architecture.

I live in Copenhagen and run my own products from there. I take on selected freelance and advisory work — usually technical leadership, architecture or applied AI. [Send me an email](/en/contact) if that sounds like what you need.
