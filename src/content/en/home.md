## About me {#about}

I have 15+ years of experience building software, much of it with startups and public institutions in Copenhagen and Berlin. I architect, build, maintain and optimise web, mobile and desktop apps — including API, webhook and payment integrations, and UX work on user flows and layouts rather than visual design.

I help companies integrate AI into existing processes and build new AI-powered services. I built [SQLAI.ai](/resume#sqlai), which has more than 150,000 users.

I set up tests, CI/CD, environments and logging to keep applications running reliably. I also lead development teams, guiding technical decisions, reviewing code and mentoring developers.

My toolkit includes Node.js and TypeScript, Python, Go, PHP and SQL. I choose [languages and technologies](/technologies) for the problem rather than defining my work by one stack.
