I build software across the stack, from the first architectural decisions to shipping applications that stay fast and maintainable.

## About me {#about}

I have 15+ years of experience building software, much of it with startups and public institutions in Copenhagen and Berlin. I architect, build, maintain and optimize web, mobile and desktop apps — from frontend UX and data abstractions to backend data modeling, type-safe APIs, third-party integrations and deployment.

Since 2023 I have worked with startups to build and launch four AI products, and I work extensively with applied AI — RAG, agentic workflows and prompt design. I help companies integrate AI into existing processes and build new AI-powered services. I helped build [SQLAI.ai](/resume#sqlai), which has more than 150,000 users.

I set up tests, CI/CD, environments and logging to keep applications running reliably. I also lead development teams, guiding technical decisions, reviewing code and mentoring developers.

My toolkit includes Node.js and TypeScript, Python, Go, PHP and SQL. I choose [languages and technologies](/technologies) for the problem rather than defining my work by one stack.

## How I work {#approach}

I'm pragmatic and get stuff done, and I enjoy working with people from different backgrounds — preferably on site with your team in Copenhagen. I like finding creative solutions to complex problems.

I take on selected freelance and consulting projects and am open to startup ideas. [Get in touch](/contact).
